# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Paper Shell serves everyday and long-form writers who want a lightweight desktop editor that stays out of the way while they think and write. They need a calm writing surface and fast access to document context.

## Product Purpose

Paper Shell is a compact, informative writing editor for uninterrupted drafting and revision. Success means the writer remains in control and can focus on thinking and writing without distractions.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, quiet, trustworthy. The product should feel like a dependable writing instrument rather than a bloated application or a decorative spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Bloated writing tools that prioritize decorative features over the writer's own thinking.
- Generic SaaS dashboards with decorative cards, metrics, and visual noise.
- Interfaces that hide actions, mutate documents without confirmation, or add unnecessary complexity.

## Design Principles

- Keep the document primary. The interface stays out of the way of the writing surface.
- Preserve authorship. The writer remains in complete control of their words.
- Earn trust through restraint. Use familiar controls, concise copy, and predictable behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA-equivalent interaction quality for the desktop UI: keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, non-color-only status communication, and reduced or nonessential motion.
